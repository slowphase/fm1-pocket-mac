FM-1 Pocket: opening it the first time
======================================

FM-1 Pocket is not sold through the App Store and is not "notarised" by Apple (that needs a paid Apple developer
account). macOS therefore blocks it the first time. You only have to do this once.

1. Drag "FM-1 Pocket" onto the Applications shortcut in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "FM-1 Pocket".
3. macOS says it cannot verify the app. Click "Done" (or "OK"). Do NOT click "Move to Bin".
4. Open System Settings, then click "Privacy & Security" in the sidebar.
5. Scroll down to the "Security" section. You will see a line saying "FM-1 Pocket" was blocked.
   Click "Open Anyway".
6. Type your Mac password (or use Touch ID), then click "Open Anyway" once more.

From then on it opens normally, like any other app.

The step in System Settings only appears for about an hour after you first try to open the app. If you cannot see
it, double-click the app again and go straight back to Privacy & Security.

Alternative, for people comfortable with Terminal: paste this line and press Return, then open the app normally.

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/FM-1 Pocket.app"

When you install a newer version, drag it into Applications and choose "Replace". You may need to repeat the steps
above once for the new version.
