**A universe of firmware. One little player.**

A small update to FM-1 Pocket for macOS.

## What's changed

- **The faceplate matches the real FM-1.** The four controls on the left are now where they are on the device: MASTER and SELECT on top, PRESETS and ALGORITHM below, with OCT− and OCT+ underneath. They work exactly as before, keyboard shortcuts included.
- **Firmware that shares a package now installs properly.** Two firmwares built from the same original package (Argo and Felucca 8 Patterns) each get their own download, offline copy and saved settings, instead of one of them sometimes failing to install.

## Install

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

1. Download `FM1-Pocket-0.9.1.zip` below and double-click it if your Mac hasn't unzipped it already.
2. Drag **FM-1 Pocket** into your **Applications** folder, replacing the old version. Your library, favourites and saved settings stay as they are.
3. Open it. FM-1 Pocket isn't notarised by Apple, so macOS may block it when first opened: click **Done**, then **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**. [Step-by-step help](https://github.com/slowphase/fm1-pocket-mac/blob/main/docs/If%20the%20app%20will%20not%20open.txt).
