**More firmware to discover, with credit where it's due.**

FM-1 Pocket 0.9.1 gets the library ready for many more firmware listings, from FM1 Editor's community directory.

## What's new

- **More firmware under More Firmware.** FM-1 Pocket now also lists firmware from [FM1 Editor](https://fm1-editor.com/firmware/)'s directory. You can read about these and open their source pages, but they can't be played in FM-1 Pocket until they've been reviewed.
- **Descriptions with their credit.** A description written by FM1 Editor shows "Description from FM1 Editor" and its licence underneath. Firmware listed by FM1 Editor also shows "Listed by FM1 Editor", linked to its page there (also in the right-click menu).
- **Favourites for listed firmware.** You can favourite firmware under More Firmware too, with the star in its details or the right-click menu. If it becomes playable later, it stays in your Favourites.
- **Steadier library.** A firmware keeps its place, favourite and settings when another source starts offering it, even on a day the app can only reach the basic catalogue.

## Install

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

1. Download `FM1-Pocket-0.9.1.zip` below and double-click it if your Mac hasn't unzipped it already.
2. Drag **FM-1 Pocket** into your **Applications** folder, replacing the old version. Your library, favourites and saved settings are kept.
3. Open it. FM-1 Pocket isn't notarised by Apple, so macOS may block it when first opened: click **Done**, then **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**. [Step-by-step help](https://github.com/slowphase/fm1-pocket-mac/blob/main/docs/If%20the%20app%20will%20not%20open.txt).

Prefer not to install anything? Try the [web player](https://slowphase.github.io/fm1-pocket-player/).
