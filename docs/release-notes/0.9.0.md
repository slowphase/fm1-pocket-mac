**A universe of firmware. One little player.**

The first release of FM-1 Pocket for macOS: a native Mac app for discovering and auditioning M-VAVE FM-1 community firmware. No FM-1 hardware required.

## What's in it

- **A library that keeps itself up to date**, with screen previews, descriptions and firmware details. Browse by category, search by name, maker or description, sort by latest release or A to Z, and see what's New to Library.
- **Two ways to play**: original `.fwsc` packages, exactly as their makers released them, on the fm1-emu emulator, and `.rfm1` builds. FM-1 Pocket recommends an engine for each firmware, and you can switch where both work.
- **The full FM-1 faceplate**: screen, knobs or sliders, buttons, lights and keys. Play with the mouse, your computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard.
- **Favourites and Recently Played**, offline play for anything you've downloaded, and settings kept between sessions where the firmware saves them.
- **Honest compatibility**: each firmware says whether it has passed automated checks, been listened to, or has a known issue. Most firmware has passed automated checks only so far.

## Install

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

1. Download `FM1-Pocket-0.9.0.zip` below and double-click it if your Mac hasn't unzipped it already.
2. Drag **FM-1 Pocket** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. FM-1 Pocket isn't notarised by Apple, so macOS may block it when first opened: click **Done**, then **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**. [Step-by-step help](https://github.com/slowphase/fm1-pocket-mac/blob/main/docs/If%20the%20app%20will%20not%20open.txt).

Prefer not to install anything? Try the [web player](https://slowphase.github.io/fm1-pocket-player/).
